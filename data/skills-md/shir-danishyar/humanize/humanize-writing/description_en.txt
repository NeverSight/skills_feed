Use when writing or editing prose an audience will read — emails, blog posts, articles, marketing and website copy, LinkedIn or social posts, cover letters, newsletters, product descriptions, reports, essays — and when the user asks to humanize text, make it sound natural or less robotic, remove AI patterns, or clean up an AI-generated draft. Applies by default to any audience-facing writing task. Do not use for code, commit messages, config files, or legal documents where formulaic precision is required.
