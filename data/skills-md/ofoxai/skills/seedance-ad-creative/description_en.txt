Generate a cinematic brand/product ad clip from a product description or photo using the Ofox video API (Seedance 2.5) — writes a shot-craft prompt (product framing, camera language, brand tone), shows a cost estimate, then calls ofox-video-core to submit, poll, download, and report the real cost. Use when a user asks for a commercial-style product or brand video, e.g. "give this perfume bottle a 10-second cinematic brand ad", "make a product ad for our new sneaker", "turn this product photo into a hero video for the landing page", or "I need a 15-second brand video with a slow orbit around the bottle". Do not use for dialogue-driven scenes with people talking (see seedance-short-drama).
